point of the feature:** that cadence only fires when updates are arriving, and re-renders only the
keys queued by those updates. The exact situation staleness exists to reveal — a message going
quiet, or the entire link dropping — produces no updates at all, so nothing would ever re-render
and nothing would ever dim. Record this deviation and its reason in the summary.
