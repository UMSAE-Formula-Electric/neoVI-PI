  <name>Checkpoint: Pre-register the pass threshold and lock the modelling assumptions</name>
  <decision>The pre-registered pass threshold for the compression gate, plus four modelling assumptions the planner was asked to propose and the user to confirm.</decision>
  <context>
D-13 says the threshold is written down *before* the measurement runs, and CONTEXT.md's "Claude's Discretion" section leaves the specific number to the planner to propose and the user to confirm. D-14 says the typical entropy bracket gates the verdict and the worst-case bracket informs it, and rates that verdict **one-way**: every later phase's link scope is built against it.

Four further items need confirming in the same reply, because each one moves every combined-bus number the gate reports:

1. **Batch size.** RESEARCH.md Assumption A3 proposes **100 frames per batch** (about 25 ms at the combined ~4,000 frames/s target). It sits inside the 20-50 ms flush cadence the project architecture already recommends, and keeps the CFR's uint16 `delta_t_us` field (max 65,535 µs) comfortably inside its range. D-06 requires a single stated batch size; sweeping it is deferred to Phase 1/5.

2. **Bus assignment.** RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 is the largest gap: `epbr2026_DBC_v1.0.dbc` has one `BU_:` node line and **no per-message bus attribute at all**, so nothing in this repository says which of the 128 messages rides HSCAN (~2,900 frames/s) versus HSCAN2 (~830 frames/s). Proposed placeholder, by ECU sender, flagged `confirmed: false` in the config and surfaced in the report:
   - **bus1**: `VEHICLE_CONTROL_UNIT`, `MOTOR_CONTROLLER`, `INERTIAL_MEASUREMENT_UNIT`, `SENSOR_CONTROL_UNIT`, and any message with no declared sender
   - **bus2**: `EMUS_BMS`, `ACCUMULATOR_CONTROL_UNIT`, `ENERGY_METER`, `U2C`
   If the team already knows the real wiring, say so here and it goes in as `confirmed: true`.

3. **Worst-N.** RESEARCH.md Open Question 3 proposes **N = 10** individually ranked worst IDs alongside the four categorical buckets — enough to catch outliers that fit no clean category, small enough to read as one table.

4. **Entropy bracket definitions.** D-02 requires three brackets reported as a band. Proposed, expressed as the knobs the generator actually reads:
   - **best** — analog signals dither 1 low bit with probability 0.05 per frame; discrete/enum signals change with probability 0.002; counters increment
   - **typical** — analog signals dither 2 low bits with probability 0.50; discrete signals change with probability 0.010; counters increment
   - **worst** — analog signals re-randomize 4 low bits every frame (probability 1.00); discrete signals change with probability 0.100; counters increment
