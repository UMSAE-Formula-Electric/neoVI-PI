- **`OpsConfig` lives in `src/telemetry/logger/config.py`, NOT a new `src/telemetry/ops/config.py`**
  (a deviation from RESEARCH.md's suggestion). An `ops/config.py` would need `LoggerConfigError`
  and the `_require_*` helpers from `logger/config.py`, which would in turn need `OpsConfig` back —
  a circular import. 03-CONTEXT.md also forbids a second config mechanism.
- **Exactly five heartbeat states.** A clean stop is signalled by the status file's *absence*, not
  a sixth state — a stale `capturing` file left behind would read to a human as a failure.

  `archive_write_failure`). Zero frames/s does **not** map to degraded: a parked car with the bus
  unpowered legitimately produces no frames, and a false `degraded` would train the team to ignore
  the indicator.

  bring-up section. Its entire content is "starts automatically on power-up", which no
