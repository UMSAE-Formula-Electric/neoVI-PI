  <files>src/telemetry/capture/replay_source.py, tests/test_replay_source.py</files>
  <read_first>src/telemetry/wire/frame_source.py, src/telemetry/archive/reader.py, src/telemetry/archive/record.py, src/telemetry/synth/generator.py</read_first>
  <behavior>
Written in `tests/test_replay_source.py`, replacing 01-02's placeholder skip. Every test
writes a real archive through the real `ArchiveWriter` first, so the round trip is proven
against the format actually produced rather than a hand-built file.

- a session of two hundred frames across both configured channels, written and closed, then
  replayed: `frames()` yields exactly two hundred `RawFrame`s whose id, extended flag,
  payload, and timestamp are identical to what was written, in the order they were written
- `captured_frames()` yields the same frames tagged with the channel name they were archived
  under, recovered from the archive channel's topic
- a channel filter narrows which channels are yielded without changing any yielded frame
- a crashed session — written with the writer's finish deliberately never called — replays the
  records that were completely written, because the source reads through the recovery path
  rather than through a reader that assumes a complete file
- the source is a valid `FrameSource`: assert it satisfies the protocol structurally, and
  assert the frames it yields feed `Encoder.encode` without modification, since the whole
  point of the D-08 interface is that the encoder and the measurement tool need no change
- a measured frame-rate helper reports per-channel frames per second computed from the
  archive's own timestamps, and its total matches the frame count divided by the archive's
  time span
  </behavior>
  <action>
Create `src/telemetry/capture/replay_source.py` holding `ArchiveReplaySource`, the third
implementation of the `FrameSource` protocol after the synthetic generator and the hardware
source. `src/telemetry/wire/frame_source.py`'s own module docstring names this reader as the
deferred Phase 1 implementation the protocol was formalised for — this is that.

Read through `archive/reader.py`'s recovery entry point rather than opening the file directly.
Two reasons, both load-bearing: the byte layout of an archived record is defined once in
`archive/record.py` and must not be re-derived here, and a session recorded during a real
track run may well be one that lost power, so the replay source must handle a footer-less
archive by construction rather than by exception.

Mirror `SyntheticFrameSource`'s shape: `captured_frames()` as the primary generator yielding
frames tagged with the channel they were archived under, `frames()` as a thin delegate that
yields the `RawFrame` half for protocol compatibility, and an optional channel filter that
narrows what is yielded without altering anything that is.

Add a helper that reports measured per-channel frames per second computed from the archive's
own first and last timestamps. Phase 0's rate model was a prose-sourced assumption marked
unconfirmed in `config/gate.json`; an archive of real traffic is the only thing that answers
it, and the number should come out of the log rather than out of a config field.
  </action>

  </verify>
  <done>
